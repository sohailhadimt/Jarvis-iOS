# Licences

ASG HQ does not ship third-party art, audio, 3D models, fonts, or a game engine.

- The office, apartment, rooftop, characters, furniture, hologram screens, app icon, and skyline are procedural. They are built at runtime from SceneKit primitives (boxes, spheres, cylinders, cones, and planes) and images drawn with UIKit. That artwork is original to this project.
- The interface uses Apple SF Symbols and the iOS system font, which are part of the iOS SDK.
- Frameworks are SwiftUI, UIKit, SceneKit, Speech, AVFoundation, and Security. They ship with Xcode. There is no Unity, Unreal, or paid asset pack.

Nothing was downloaded from an asset store, so there is no separate CC0 notice to attach. The procedural source is the licence record.
